Sample reflections on common early-career manager mistakes.

Some patterns I’ve seen (and repeated) as a new manager:

- **Calendar as comfort blanket.** Filling the week with 1:1s and status meetings, but not carving out time
  for thinking about org design, interfaces, and where the team should be in 6–12 months.
- **Unclear edges.** Not being explicit about what the team owns, which leads to “helping with everything”
  and shipping nothing.
- **Invisible expectations.** Assuming reports know what “good” looks like without ever writing it down.

In a real piece I’d slow down and unpack one of these; for now, this text is here so you can see how
a slightly longer career post renders in the list view and the single-post page.
