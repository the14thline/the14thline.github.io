This is a medium sample post to test headings, bullets, and spacing.

## The goal

My goal with reading notes isn’t to summarize; it’s to **leave myself a trail** so I can pick up the idea later.

## A simple template

- **One sentence**: what is this about?
- **Three bullets**: what changed in my mind?
- **Quote/snippet**: the line I’ll remember.
- **Next action**: a thing to try, build, or look up.

## What I avoid

- Copy-pasting too much text.
- Notes that have no opinion (“interesting!”) but no decision.
- Making a note for everything instead of for what matters.
