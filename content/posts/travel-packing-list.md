Medium sample post to test list layout and spacing.

## Always-pack items

- Passport / ID
- Wallet + one backup card
- Phone + charger
- Earbuds
- A pen (surprisingly useful)

## Optional but high leverage

- A tiny power strip
- Packing cubes
- A tote bag
- A light jacket even in warm places

## Personal rule

If I can’t explain why I’m packing an item in one sentence, it probably stays home.
