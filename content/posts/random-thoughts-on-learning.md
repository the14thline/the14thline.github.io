Loose collection of thoughts on learning; used here to test scroll length and archive views.

Some half-baked beliefs I keep coming back to:

- The best **learning loops** are short, cheap, and a little bit fun.
- You only really understand a tool once you’ve used it on a project that mattered to you.
- Rereading your own old notes is underrated; you get a free conversation with a past version of yourself.

On this site I’d like “learning” posts to be a mix of:

1. concrete notes from experiments or projects,
2. references to papers, books, or talks that actually changed my mind,
3. and small invitations – e.g. “try this prompt”, “implement this toy version”, “watch for this pattern this week”.

which ideas you actually want to write up first.
