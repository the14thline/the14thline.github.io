A sample post in **Career → Leadership**.

Early leadership mistakes tend to rhyme across teams and companies:

- trying to be “the expert” instead of creating space for the team’s expertise,
- over-focusing on individual output instead of system constraints,
- and avoiding hard feedback conversations until they explode.

If I were turning this into a real post, I’d probably:

1. Tell one or two concrete stories (with anonymized details).
2. Extract the pattern behind the mistake.
3. Offer a small, testable behavior change you can try next week.

Here it functions mostly as a **career**-flavored article with a few paragraphs and bullets
so that the “career” category page doesn’t look empty.
