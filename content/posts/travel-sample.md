A sample post in **Lifestyle → Travel**.

If I’m honest with myself, my favorite trips are usually the low-key ones:
good coffee, long walks, and just enough novelty that my brain notices details again.

When I write about travel I usually try to capture three things:

- **Texture** – what it *felt* like to be there (light, sound, pace), not just a list of places.
- **Routines** – where I ended up going twice, what I actually ate, how I started the day.
- **Logistics notes** – 3–5 concrete tips I’d send a friend if they were visiting next month.

This post is just placeholder content, but you can imagine it turning into a real city guide.
For our purposes, it gives the “Lifestyle” category something non-trivial to render on the home page,
in the archives, and in the category listing.
