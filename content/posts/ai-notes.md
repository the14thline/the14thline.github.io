A sample post in **Tech → AI**.

Over the last decade I’ve accumulated a messy pile of AI notes across papers, internal docs, and side projects.
This blog is where I try to compress that pile into something readable for a future version of myself.
I don’t aim to be exhaustive or original on every topic; instead I try to answer one concrete question
per post, with enough pointers that you can go deeper on your own.

When I say “AI” here I mostly mean:

- modern deep learning (Transformers and friends),
- practical ML systems work (training, serving, eval, observability),
- and alignment-ish concerns that show up when you put models in the real world.

Some rough principles I want these notes to follow:

1. **Write for future-me** – assume I’ve forgotten the details but still remember the shape of the idea.
2. **Pin down definitions** – a surprising amount of disagreement comes from fuzzy terminology.
3. **Show failure modes** – real systems break in specific, boring ways; those stories are often the most useful.

This post itself is just scaffolding so we can test how a medium-length article renders in the layout,
but the structure is the same as I’d use for a real AI note: short intro, a few sharp bullets, and links out.
