A sample post in **Thoughts**.

I don’t have a grand theory of writing; mostly I’ve noticed that when I stop writing for a while,
my thinking gets fuzzier. Drafts are where I notice the gaps in my understanding.

Reasons I want this blog to exist:

- **Memory.** Past-me forgets almost everything except emotion and vague shapes of ideas.
  Notes like these give future-me a chance to reconstruct the details.
- **Compression.** Forcing a concept into a few pages is a good test of whether I actually grok it.
- **Sharing.** Sometimes the right paragraph at the right time can unblock someone else.

Even if no one reads a given post, the act of writing it usually pays for itself.
In the context of this site, this particular article also plays a practical role:
it’s a medium-length, text-heavy page in the **thoughts** category that helps us see how
reflection-style essays will feel in the theme.
