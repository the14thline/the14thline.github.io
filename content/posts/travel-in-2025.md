Sample post about travel plans to test how long lists of posts look.

For 2025 I’d like my travel to be a bit more intentional:

1. **One deep trip** – stay in a place for 3–4 weeks, learn the transit map, develop a routine.
2. **One family trip** – optimize for shared memories, not productivity.
3. **One solo reset** – somewhere quiet, with just enough friction that I remember how to be bored.

From a blog perspective, long travel posts stress-test:

- image handling and captions,
- long sections with nested headings,
- and how the site behaves when a single article is 10–20 minutes of reading.

The actual travel content can evolve later; right now this gives us another reasonably-sized page
to scroll through and to see in the archive grouped under “lifestyle”.
